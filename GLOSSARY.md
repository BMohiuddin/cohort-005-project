# Cadence

A platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Language

### Buying

**Purchase**:
The record of a learner paying for a course, including the amount they paid.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, made on the course page with a reason, to get their money back for a Purchase. It is Requested until the team Approves or Declines it. A learner whose request was Declined may raise another while the Purchase still has Refund Eligibility.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when their Refund Request is Approved. A Declined request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised right now: inside the Refund Window, below the watch and completion limits, with no claimed team seats, and with no Refund Request already Requested.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
