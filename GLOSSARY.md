# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, customer

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: The team, staff (for this meaning), Team Admin

**Team Admin**:
The person who manages a Team Licence and its Seats. Not an Admin.

### Team Licences

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of a course for several people, handed out as Seats.

**Seat**:
One person's place on a Team Licence, claimed by redeeming its code. An unclaimed Seat can be voided so it can never be claimed.
_Avoid_: Coupon (for this meaning)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin other than the requester approves or declines it, and that decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin hasn't decided yet. While it is open, no Seat on its purchase can be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it leads to a Refund and, at that moment, takes away only what that purchase gave: the learner's access for a personal purchase, or the unclaimed Seats for a Team Licence. The learner's progress is kept, and comes back if they buy the course again.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request: they must be inside the Refund Window and under both the Watch Limit and the Completion Limit. Crossing any one of them ends eligibility. It is judged only when the request is raised, never again when it is decided, and counts all the learner's progress in the course, from any purchase.

**Refund Window**:
Fourteen calendar days from purchase, including the whole of the fourteenth day.

**Watch Limit**:
Two hours of the course's video watched.

**Completion Limit**:
Half of the course's lessons completed.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
